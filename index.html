3. Friction Fixer Workbook (spreadsheet template)

A method for finding tools that fix a specific struggle. It breaks a struggle into research-named friction points, matches each one to its best-supported treatment, and pairs each treatment with a second treatment that prevents its side effects. Then it audits apps, devices, and services against those treatments by cost, setup effort, privacy, and the person’s own devices. Every mark cites a dated source.
